I'm an applied AI researcher and engineer, and a KTP Associate at Cold Banana, with a PhD in artificial intelligence and more than five years of work on real-time computer vision, GPU and edge AI, and agentic systems. I also remain a Research Fellow in the School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science at Queen Mary University of London. My work centres on building and deploying production AI platforms with NVIDIA technologies, deep learning, large language models, and scalable ML infrastructure.

At Cold Banana I lead a GPU-accelerated multi-camera analytics platform on NVIDIA DeepStream and Jetson Orin, covering detection, tracking, facial expression, and gesture recognition, and I am architecting an enterprise agentic RAG platform with TurboVec, tool orchestration, memory, grounding, and guardrails. Alongside that, my research at Queen Mary has focused on efficient 3D object detection, CUDA optimisation, and distributed training for large-scale sensor data.

I have taught and supervised postgraduate students in machine learning, big data, and computer vision, including a QMUL nomination to lecture Introduction to Data Science at QUPT in Hainan. I am motivated by systems that combine research depth with production engineering.
